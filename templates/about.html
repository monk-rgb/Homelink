03 · ABOUT HOMELINK

Your link to smarter property decisions.

Homelink brings valuation, discovery and day-to-day property operations together in one calm, premium workspace. It is built for the Nigerian market: fast enough for browsing, structured enough for business workflows, and honest about the limits of an automated estimate.

WHAT WE DO

Valuation you can trust

Homelink turns the details of a property — location, type, bedrooms, bathrooms, area, age and parking — into a clear, data-driven price estimate in seconds, so you can act with confidence.

DISCOVERY

Verified listings, nationwide

Browse sale and rental properties from verified sellers across Nigeria on PropkoNet. Filter by state, area, price, property type and more, then connect directly with the seller.

FOR BUSINESS

An operations workspace

Property owners and agents get a dedicated workspace built around leads, listings, deals, tasks and AI-assisted documents — everything needed to run property business in one place.

HOW IT WORKS

Better estimates start with better data.

Homelink is trained on a dataset of 1,000 Nigerian property records spanning states and property types. Each estimate is transparent about its confidence range, and its holdout accuracy metrics are recorded in metrics.json. Visual estimates from property photos are indicative and never a substitute for a physical inspection or a licensed valuer.
